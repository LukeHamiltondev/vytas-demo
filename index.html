What we fix

Servicing and repairs, from a warning light to a worn clutch. If your job isn't listed, ring and ask.

  • Servicing

    Interim and full services, oil and filters, and the checks that keep a car out of trouble between them.

    • Oil & filter
    • Full service
    • Timing belts
    • Fluids & plugs

  • Diagnostics

    Warning light on? The fault codes get read and the cause traced, so you know what it actually needs.

    • Engine light
    • ABS & airbag lights
    • Electrical faults

  • Brakes & suspension

    Pads, discs, shocks and springs, and the squeals, knocks and pulls that come with them.

    • Pads & discs
    • Shock absorbers
    • Springs
    • Bushes & links

  • Clutch & gearbox

    A slipping clutch, a heavy pedal or gears that crunch going in.

    • Clutch replacement
    • Flywheels
    • Gearbox faults

  • Tyres & wheels

    Punctures, worn tyres and wheels that need balancing.

    • Puncture repair
    • New tyres
    • Balancing

  • NCT prep

    A once-over before the test, and the repairs after a fail so the car is ready for its retest.

    • Pre-NCT check
    • Fail-sheet repairs
