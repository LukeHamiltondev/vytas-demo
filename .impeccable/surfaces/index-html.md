# Home page (index.html)

Scope: the whole one-page demo site. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: local drivers with a car problem, mostly on a phone; second reader is Vytas, who must recognise his garage and want the site.
Action: ring the garage (one tap), or send a booking request.
Proof on hand: none yet; every fact Vytas has not supplied is a visible `.ph` placeholder.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS, no build, Vercel; English only; reduced motion respected.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the workshop's shadow board, where every tool hangs in its own painted outline. Each service is a tool in its place, so the board says "an orderly owner-run workshop" without a single adjective. It refuses the category default of dark asphalt photo hero, hi-vis orange buttons and a row of equal service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Hammerite machinery-green pegboard with a real hole grid as the ground; flat white painted silhouettes; steel-grey tools with red dipped handles; black embossed label-tape for names; stencil signwriting for the display voice (Big Shoulders Stencil), Big Shoulders for headings, Archivo for reading text. Reading sections sit on pale cool galvanised grey, never cream.

STORY: The visitor sees the garage name signwritten on the board and the phone number on a hanging tag, recognises the jobs they need among the tools, taps one to read what it covers, and rings or books.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed green board. Across the top hang six tools in their outlines, each with a label-tape name; they are the service navigation. Below, the name is signwritten large across the board, a one-line offer, and a red call tag plus a "Book a repair" tag hung on hooks. Mobile: the tools become a 3x2 rack above the name; a sticky call bar appears after the hero.

FORM: Shadow board, candidate 7 of 7 on my ordered list (job card, shutter signwriting, Haynes manual, Irish road signs, warning lights, NCT disc, shadow board); seed key 8215b093 (degraded roll, no challengers).

Signature interaction: hovering or focusing a tool lifts it off its hook so the painted outline shows beneath; the motion is a short swing with exponential ease-out, skipped under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
